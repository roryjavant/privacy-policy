Privacy Policy

App: RantDrop
Developer: Rory Avant
Effective date: February 27, 2026

Quick summary

  • RantDrop lets you record videos and generate AI music and transcripts.
  • We store your content (rant videos, reaction videos, generated songs, and transcripts) so you can use it in the app.
  • We use third-party services to run the app (hosting/auth/storage) and to provide AI features (music generation, transcription).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

RantDrop is developed and operated by Rory Avant (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the RantDrop mobile application (the “App”).

Information we collect

1) Account information

The App uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. We receive a unique identifier for your account and may receive an email relay address if you choose Apple’s “Hide My Email” option.

2) User content you provide

To provide core features, we collect and store content you create or upload, which may include:

  • Rant videos
  • Reaction videos
  • AI-generated songs
  • Transcripts generated from your audio (e.g., via speech-to-text)

3) Purchases and token usage

If you purchase tokens or other in-app purchases, Apple processes payment information. We may receive purchase-related data (e.g., product ID, purchase status, transaction identifiers) to deliver purchases and prevent fraud.

4) Device and app data (limited)

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the App, such as device identifiers, app version, and basic logs for debugging and reliability. We aim to minimize collection of non-essential data.

Permissions (Camera & Microphone)

RantDrop requires access to your device camera and microphone so you can record rant and reaction videos. You can manage these permissions in your device settings. If you deny permissions, some features may not work.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and operate the App’s core features (recording, storing, and playing back your content)
  • Generate AI features you request, such as songs and transcripts
  • Process token usage and deliver in-app purchases
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Improve reliability and performance

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the App and deliver features, including with these categories of service providers:

Service providers

  • Supabase (authentication, database, and storage) — used to store and serve App data and your content.
  • AI / processing providers — used to provide features you request, such as:
    • ElevenLabs (AI music generation for songs)
    • OpenAI Whisper (speech-to-text transcription)
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases are provided through Apple.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the App.

Data retention

We keep your account information and stored content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. You may request deletion of your account and associated content (subject to legal obligations and legitimate business needs such as fraud prevention and transaction recordkeeping).

Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect your information. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

RantDrop is not intended for children under 13 (or the age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your choices

  • Permissions: You can control camera/microphone access in device settings.
  • Account and content: You can request deletion of your account and associated stored content by contacting us.

International users

If you use the App outside the United States, your information may be processed and stored in countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice within the App.
