Privacy Policy

App: RantDrop
Developer: Rory Avant
Effective date: February 27, 2026

Quick summary

  • RantDrop lets you record videos and generate AI music and transcripts.
  • Content you create in the app is stored locally on your device.
  • When you use AI features, necessary audio or text may be sent to third-party AI providers to generate songs or transcripts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

RantDrop is developed and operated by Rory Avant (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the RantDrop mobile application (the “App”).

Information we collect

1) Account information

RantDrop supports Sign in with Apple for authentication. When you sign in, we receive a unique identifier associated with your Apple account. If you choose Apple's “Hide My Email” option, we may receive a private relay email address.

2) User content you create

To provide the core features of the App, you may create content including:

  • Rant videos
  • Reaction videos
  • AI-generated songs
  • Transcripts generated from audio

This content is stored locally on your device unless it is temporarily sent to a third-party service to perform an AI feature you request.

3) Purchases and token usage

If you purchase tokens or other in-app purchases, Apple processes payment information. We may receive limited purchase information such as product identifiers and transaction status to confirm and deliver purchases.

4) Device and app information

We may collect limited technical information necessary for the operation of the App, such as:

  • App version
  • Device type
  • Basic diagnostic logs used to improve reliability and performance

Permissions (Camera and Microphone)

RantDrop requires access to your device’s camera and microphone so you can record rant and reaction videos.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings at any time. If permissions are denied, some features of the App may not function.

How we use information

We use information only to operate the App and provide requested features, including:

  • Recording and playing back videos you create
  • Generating AI songs and transcripts when requested
  • Processing token usage and delivering in-app purchases
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse
  • Improving reliability and performance of the App

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only when necessary to provide features you request.

Service providers

  • AI processing providers — used to generate features requested by the user, such as:
    • ElevenLabs for AI music generation
    • Speech-to-text providers such as Whisper for transcription
  • Apple — provides platform services including Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or security of users and the App.

Data retention

Content created within RantDrop is stored locally on your device and remains there until you delete it or remove the App.

If content is temporarily sent to third-party providers to generate AI features you request, those providers may process the data according to their own privacy policies.

Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect your information. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

RantDrop is not intended for children under 13 (or the age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Permissions: Camera and microphone permissions can be controlled in device settings.
  • Local content: You can delete content directly from your device or by removing the App.

International users

If you use the App outside the United States, information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes occur, we will update the effective date above.
