Privacy Policy

How StudySong handles your notes, songs, and account data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information StudySong collects or processes, how it is used, when it is shared with service providers, and what options users have for controlling their data.

Last updated: April 8, 2026

What StudySong does Turns notes into memory-focused songs and study prompts, with optional OCR, speech input, and account sync.
What StudySong does not do StudySong does not sell personal information and does not use ad or tracking SDKs in the app.
How to reach support Use the support link at the bottom of this page for privacy or deletion requests.
1. Information We Process

Depending on which features you use, StudySong may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information. If you sign in, StudySong uses Sign in with Apple through Supabase authentication. We may receive identifiers associated with your account and, if Apple provides it, your email address.
  • Study content you provide. This includes typed notes, dictated notes, uploaded or captured note images, course names, quiz answers, and feedback you create while using the app.
  • Generated content. StudySong may store generated song titles, choruses, mnemonic lines, study tips, source highlights, generated question sets, quiz results, review history, and progress stats tied to your account.
  • Local media and drafts. The app can store drafts and downloaded song audio on your device so you can review content locally.
  • Technical and diagnostic data. We may process request identifiers, basic error details, and service logs needed to keep the app working, investigate failures, and protect the service.
2. How We Collect Information
  • Directly from you. When you type notes, capture note photos, speak notes aloud, assign songs to courses, or answer quizzes.
  • From device permissions. If you allow it, StudySong can access the camera, microphone, and speech recognition features needed for note capture and dictation.
  • Through service providers. Authentication, database storage, OCR, AI content generation, and music generation are handled with third-party infrastructure described below.
3. How We Use Information

StudySong uses processed information to:

  • authenticate users and keep study content linked to the correct account;
  • extract text from note images;
  • generate songs, mnemonics, study questions, and quiz feedback;
  • save courses, review states, quiz attempts, and study progress;
  • store audio locally on your device for playback;
  • troubleshoot bugs, secure the service, and monitor reliability.
4. Providers and Sharing

StudySong shares data only as needed to operate the app, comply with law, or protect users and the service. Current providers reflected in the app code include:

Supabase Used for authentication, database storage, and Edge Functions that power hosted StudySong features.
OpenAI Used through StudySong Edge Functions for OCR and AI-generated study content such as song structure, questions, and scoring.
ElevenLabs Used through StudySong Edge Functions to create generated song audio.
Apple Used for Sign in with Apple and for device-level microphone, camera, and speech-recognition services.

StudySong does not sell personal information and does not use third-party advertising SDKs. If we add new providers that receive user data, we will update this policy.

5. Camera, Microphone, and Speech
  • Camera. If you choose to scan notes, StudySong uses camera or photo-library access to capture the image you selected.
  • Microphone. If you choose speech input, StudySong uses microphone access so you can dictate notes or spoken answers.
  • Speech recognition. Speech recognition in StudySong uses Apple's speech-recognition APIs. Depending on the device, language, and operating-system behavior, audio may be processed by Apple. StudySong mainly uses the resulting transcription text inside the app workflow.
  • OCR path. Note images may be processed on-device with Apple Vision or sent to StudySong's hosted OCR service, which uses OpenAI, depending on availability and app configuration.
6. Retention and Deletion
  • On-device data. Local drafts and downloaded song audio remain on your device until you remove them, clear local data in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Account-linked data. Songs, notes, courses, quiz attempts, and study progress saved through the hosted backend may remain associated with your account until they are deleted, no longer needed to operate the service, or removed following a valid request.
  • Deletion requests. If you want server-side account data deleted and the app does not yet provide the exact deletion flow you need, contact support using the link below.
7. Your Choices
  • You can choose not to use camera, microphone, or speech-recognition features.
  • You can edit extracted or dictated notes before generating study content.
  • You can clear local data from the Settings screen in the app.
  • You can contact support to ask privacy questions or request help with deletion.
8. Children's and Student Privacy

StudySong is an educational productivity app. If you are a parent, guardian, teacher, or school representative with a question about a learner's privacy, contact support and include enough detail for us to locate the relevant account or content.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version here and change the "Last updated" date at the top of the page.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: use the StudySong support channel.
